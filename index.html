R1 2026

MONETA

Research manuscript

MONETA: A Mask-Guided Self-Calibrated Framework for 1-Shot, CPU-Based BGA AOI on High-Resolution Imagery

Jaewoo Oh†, Heeyoung Ahn†, Donghee Han, Wonyong Choi, Keejun Han
† Equal contribution as co-first authors.

CPU-based BGA inspection using one normal reference image and repeated solder-ball structures. The method combines statistical peer comparison and self-calibrated scoring for high-resolution anomaly detection.

One-shot AOISegFormer-B0Statistical scoring

Research overview+

Problem. Semiconductor inspection offers limited defect examples and per-pattern normal images. High-resolution imagery also exposes illumination variation and sensor texture.

Method. An offline-trained SegFormer-B0 provides structural masks. The inspection algorithm combines solder-ball peer comparison, Grubbs-based outlier detection, self-calibrated scoring, and multiscale analysis.

Contribution. Inspection methodology, experimental evaluation, and joint manuscript preparation. The one-reference setup applies to inspection; the mask network is trained separately.

R2 2026

Anomaly Detection

Experimental research

Industrial anomaly detection with pretrained backbone features

Feature extraction · Non-parametric anomaly scoring · Benchmark evaluation

I investigate industrial anomaly detection using features extracted from frozen, pretrained backbones, without additional training or fine-tuning on the target dataset. The research focuses on comparing normal reference and query features and evaluating anomaly scoring methods.

Pretrained featuresDINOv2Anomaly detection

Research approach+

Approach. Use pretrained models such as DINOv2 as feature extractors and compute anomaly scores from relationships between normal reference and query features.

Evaluation. Compare performance across backbone sizes and input resolutions on MVTec AD and VisA.

Training scope. The backbone has been pretrained; it is not trained or fine-tuned further for the target anomaly detection task.

I have built an Isaac Sim environment representing a real manufacturing company's factory. The ongoing research connects acoustic simulation to this completed spatial environment, extending it toward a digital twin that models sound sources and listening positions.

Intended use. A simulation foundation for factory patrol, listening-position selection, and machine condition monitoring.
